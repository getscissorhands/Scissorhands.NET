# Visible Grandchild

This page appears below [Parent](parent) and the non-clickable Group label.
There is no `index.md` in the group directory, so the label has no link.

This page keeps Group visible while its sibling,
[Hidden Grandchild](parent/group/hidden-grandchild), is hidden from navigation.
Hide this page too and Group disappears. Hiding Parent also hides the entire branch.
