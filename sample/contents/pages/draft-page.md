# Draft page

This page has `draft: true`, so preview displays a draft badge while production
builds exclude it.

Because `show_in_navigation` is enabled, the page also appears in preview
navigation and the previous/next reading sequence. It remains absent from
production navigation and tag listings until you clear the draft flag.

Drafts apply to both pages and posts. Date-based scheduling applies only to
posts, so ordinary pages do not need a publication date.
